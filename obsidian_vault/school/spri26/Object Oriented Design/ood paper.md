CSCI 345 - Object Oriented Design  
Writing Proficiency Paper B – 8 Points  
Overview  
This assignment is designed to help you understand the ACM (Association for  
Computing Machinery) publication requirements for technical papers. NOTE: IF YOU  
WOULD LIKE FEEDBACK ON YOUR PAPER, THERE IS A FEEDBACK DEADLINE!  
Details  
Write a 2-3 (no more than 3) page paper that analyzes and discusses one of the 23  
design patterns outlined in “Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented  
Software”, by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, John Vlissides. The target  
audience is third year computer science majors.  
Publication Requirements  
• Papers must be completed using the ACM publication requirements and  
references must be in the ACM reference format.  
o See Canvas->Files->References->  
ACM_Submission_Template_WP_Version.docx. Base the format of your  
paper on this template.  
• Be sure to include an abstract, introduction, main body, and conclusion.  
• The paper should cite at least two sources.  
• For an example paper that conforms to the research paper requirements, see  
Canvas->Files->References->how_to_write_a_technical_paper.pdf  
• Grading will be based on content, grammar, style, format and proper editing.  
Editing involves finding grammar, spelling, punctuation, and content errors.  
• First Revision: Upload your paper to Canvas to get feedback from the grader, or  
you can meet with the grader online or in person.
