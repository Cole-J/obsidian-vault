In the above table, the {Position} attribute implies the {Phone} attribute, but the inverse {Phone} does not imply {Position} because in this case {Position} to {phone} is a one to many while {Phone} to {Position} is a many to one and this not a function.

So:
- Phone(Position) is a valid function.
- Position(Phone) is not.
