
The Cardinality of an Entity is the number of (its own) entities to which another entity can be associated via a relationship set.

In more laymen's terms, how many of the same type of an Entity Set can be associated with another amount of a different Entity Set.

One to One
One to Many
Many to One
Many to Many

Assume A and B are different Entity Sets. a1 to a4, and b1 to b4, are separate instances of the A or B entity Set
